Is Natural Gas a better fit for Emerging Economies?

macroenergy.net

Gas

14 March 2023

Natural gas offers several advantages for developing economies, including abundance, cost-effectiveness, cleanliness, and versatility. It can promote energy security, economic growth, and environmental sustainability. However, challenges such as infrastructure requirements, environmental concerns, price volatility, and carbon emissions should be carefully managed to ensure the responsible and sustainable utilization of natural gas. Developing economies must balance the benefits and drawbacks of natural gas while considering long-term energy strategies and exploring cleaner and more renewable energy options for a sustainable future.

Advantages:

Abundant and Accessible: Natural gas reserves are widely distributed globally, making it a readily available energy source for developing economies. Many developing countries have discovered significant natural gas reserves within their territories, providing them with a domestic energy supply and reducing dependence on imports. Access to abundant natural gas reserves offers long-term energy security and promotes self-sufficiency.

Cost-Effectiveness: Natural gas is often more affordable than other fossil fuels such as coal or oil. Developing economies can benefit from the cost advantages of natural gas, which can reduce energy prices for industries, businesses, and households. Lower energy costs can stimulate economic activities, attract investments, and improve living standards for the population.

Cleaner Energy Source: Compared to coal and oil, natural gas is a cleaner-burning fuel, emitting fewer pollutants and greenhouse gases when combusted. It produces significantly lower levels of sulfur dioxide, nitrogen oxides, particulate matter, and carbon dioxide. Utilizing natural gas can contribute to reducing air pollution and mitigating climate change, making it an attractive option for developing economies seeking to transition to cleaner energy sources.

Versatility and Flexibility: Natural gas can be utilized for various applications, including electricity generation, heating, cooking, and transportation. It can be easily transported through pipelines or converted into liquefied natural gas (LNG) for transportation and storage. This versatility allows developing economies to diversify their energy mix and adapt to different sectors' specific requirements, promoting energy efficiency and reliability.

Disadvantages:

Infrastructure Requirements: Developing a natural gas infrastructure involves significant investments in pipelines, storage facilities, and processing plants. Constructing and maintaining this infrastructure can be challenging and expensive for developing economies with limited financial resources and technical expertise. Insufficient infrastructure may impede the utilization of natural gas and hinder its potential benefits.

Environmental Concerns: While natural gas is cleaner than other fossil fuels, it is not entirely free from environmental concerns. The extraction process, known as hydraulic fracturing or fracking, can have adverse environmental impacts, including water contamination, methane leakage, and habitat disruption. Developing economies must implement robust regulations and safeguards to minimize these risks and ensure responsible extraction practices.

Price Volatility: Although natural gas can be cost-effective, its prices are subject to volatility. International market fluctuations, geopolitical events, and supply-demand imbalances can cause significant price variations, affecting energy affordability and economic stability. Developing economies must carefully manage the risks associated with natural gas price fluctuations to maintain energy security and avoid potential economic shocks.

Carbon Emissions: While natural gas emits less carbon dioxide than coal and oil, it still contributes to greenhouse gas emissions. In the long run, developing economies should aim for sustainable energy sources with even lower emissions or focus on transitioning to renewable energy alternatives to mitigate the impacts of climate change effectively.
