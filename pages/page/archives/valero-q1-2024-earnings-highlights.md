Valero Q1 2024 Earnings Highlights

Financial Performance

:

Net Income

: Valero reported net income attributable to Valero stockholders of

$1.2 billion

, or

$3.75 per share

, for Q1 2024. This compares to

$3.1 billion

, or

$8.29 per share

, in Q1 2023.

Adjusted Net Income

: Excluding adjustments, adjusted net income attributable to Valero stockholders was

$1.3 billion

, or

$3.82 per share

, for Q1 2024, compared to

$3.1 billion

, or

$8.27 per share

, in Q1 2023.

Refining Segment

:

The Refining segment reported operating income of

$1.7 billion

for Q1 2024, down from

$4.1 billion

in Q1 2023.

Refining throughput volumes averaged

2.8 million barrels per day

during Q1 2024 despite planned maintenance activities

.

Renewable Diesel (Diamond Green Diesel)

:

The Renewable Diesel segment, including the Diamond Green Diesel joint venture (DGD), reported

$190 million

of operating income for Q1 2024.

Segment sales volumes averaged

3.7 million gallons per day

, driven by additional volumes from the DGD Port Arthur plant

.

Ethanol Segment

:

The Ethanol segment reported

$10 million

of operating income for Q1 2024.

Ethanol production volumes averaged

4.5 million gallons per day

.

Corporate and Other

:

General and administrative expenses were

$258 million

in Q1 2024

.

Following

Valero Energy Corporation’s Q1 2024 earnings report

, the stock market had mixed reactions. Let’s delve into the details:

Earnings Per Share (EPS)

:

Valero reported

earnings per share (EPS)

of

$3.82

, which

exceeded

the analyst estimate of

$3.24

.

This positive EPS performance likely contributed to investor confidence.

Revenue

:

The company’s

revenue

for Q1 2024 was

$31.76 billion

, which

fell short

of the analyst estimate of

$33.09 billion

.

The revenue miss might have tempered market enthusiasm.

Operational Highlights

:

Refining Segment

: Valero’s refining segment reported an operating income of

$1.7 billion

for Q1 2024, a

significant decrease

from

$4.1 billion

in Q1 2023.

This decline was influenced by planned maintenance activities

.

Renewable Diesel

: The renewable diesel segment, including contributions from the Diamond Green Diesel joint venture, posted an operating income of

$190 million

.

Although slightly down from Q1 2023, this segment remained resilient

.

Ethanol Segment

: The ethanol segment saw a decrease in operating income to

$10 million

from

$39 million

, attributed to lower ethanol margins

.

Strategic Positioning

:

Valero continues to focus on operational excellence and disciplined capital investment

.

The company’s strategic updates include the SAF (sustainable aviation fuel) project at the DGD Port Arthur plant, expected to start in Q4 2024, enhancing SAF production capabilities

.
