## Iran's 'Zero Oil Exports' Claim Isn't True Yet — It Could Be by Late September

<cite index="63-6">Iran's central bank governor said this week it's "a reality that we are not exporting oil."</cite> That's premature, but the math is closing fast: <cite index="93-1,93-4,93-5">China's Iranian imports fell from 785,000 bpd in June to 823,000 in July to just 534,000 bpd so far in August.</cite> <cite index="93-10,93-11">Floating storage outside the blockade zone has dropped from 105 million to 80 million barrels, with only about 30 million left in Asian waters.</cite> <cite index="93-12">One Kpler analyst warned buyers could see virtually no new Iranian supply for late-September delivery.</cite> The zero-export claim is a forecast now — it becomes fact within weeks.

## China Is Out-Bidding India for Russian Crude — India's Imports Just Hit a War-Era Low

<cite index="92-9,92-10">China's share of Russian crude sourced from European ports jumped to 31% in August from 22% in June, moving into India's traditional supply lane.</cite> <cite index="92-11,92-12">India's imports from those ports are down almost 30%, pulling its total crude imports to the lowest level since the Iran conflict started.</cite> <cite index="57-16,57-17">India is a major exporter of diesel and gasoline into an Asian market already short of both, and crude arriving today becomes fuel tomorrow.</cite> If India can't replace those barrels, September product exports — not just Indian refiners — take the hit.
