## Saudi Arabia Is Burning Through Its Own Stockpile to Hide a Production Collapse

Riyadh told OPEC its August crude output fell 1.9 million barrels a day to 6.238 million b/d, the lowest since 1990. Yet supply to market held near 7.1 million bpd because Saudi Arabia's oil supply to the market reached 7.122 million barrels per day in August, surpassing its production — a sign it's drawing on inventories to sustain deliveries amid Hormuz and Red Sea disruptions. That buffer isn't infinite. If both export routes stay squeezed into October, the next OPEC filing could be the one that finally breaks.

## Iran-Aligned Forces Now Control Both Oil Chokepoints Flanking the Arabian Peninsula

The Houthis captured the strategic Perim Island in the Bab al-Mandeb Strait on Friday, a day after seizing the port town of Mocha, effectively completing their rapid push to take control of Yemen's Red Sea coastline. Combined with Iran's Hormuz blockade, Tehran-aligned forces now flank corridors handling more than one quarter of seaborne crude oil and petroleum trade. Saudi Arabia's Red Sea workaround — its last major export lifeline since Hormuz seized up — now sits inside Houthi missile range.
