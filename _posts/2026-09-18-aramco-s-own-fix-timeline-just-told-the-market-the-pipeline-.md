## Aramco's Own Fix Timeline Just Told the Market the Pipeline Outage Isn't a Few Days — It's Six Weeks

Update: Aramco isn't just bypassing the damaged East-West segment for a quick fix — <cite index="59-1,59-3,59-4">Saudi Arabia is seeking to return about half the capacity of its cross-country oil pipeline within days, with the company looking to return the conduit to its full capability in about six weeks</cite>. That's a materially longer timeline than the market's initial 'days' framing implied. Half capacity buys breathing room, not resolution — expect Aramco's Asia-first cargo allocation and Europe's scramble for replacement barrels to persist deep into November, not just this week.

## Refiners Are Quietly Skipping Turnarounds Right When Washington Wants Them Running Harder

The maintenance season nobody's taking: <cite index="19-11,19-14">planned crude outages for fall 2026 are 144,000 bpd lower, and fall 2027 is 283,000 bpd higher, with planned work at 10 crude units moved from fall 2026 to fall 2027</cite>, because <cite index="19-6,19-7">refineries are running high throughput rates, making refined products while the operating margins are good</cite>. That's colliding with reports that <cite index="20-6,20-7">Trump was mulling how to use the Defense Production Act to boost refinery throughput</cite> — a mandate for capacity that's already being self-rationed toward profit, not policy.

## Tuesday's UN Sideline Meeting Is Where Trump Decides Whether the Iran War Gets a Second Act

This isn't another vague 'ceasefire soon' signal — <cite index="31-1">Trump told Axios he's approaching a critical juncture over whether to restart massive attacks in an effort to bring the conflict to a close</cite>, and the venue is set: <cite index="28-1">Trump is planning to meet Gulf leaders on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly in New York next Tuesday to discuss next steps for the war with Iran</cite>. Crude's three-day slide assumes de-escalation. A resumption signal out of that room reprices Hormuz risk within hours, not weeks.
